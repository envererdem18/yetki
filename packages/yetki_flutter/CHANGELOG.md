## 0.1.0

- Initial release. Adds:
  - `YetkiScope`, with the `context.yetki` and `context.can` helpers.
  - `PermissionGuard`, `RoleGuard`, `YetkiGuard` and `YetkiBuilder`
    widgets.
  - `YetkiListenable`, for go_router's `refreshListenable` and similar
    APIs.
  - `SharedPreferencesYetkiStorage`, which reads the yetki 0.1.x cache key.
